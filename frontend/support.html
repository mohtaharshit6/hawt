— 02 / returns & exchanges

Returns & Exchanges

Changed your mind? Wrong size? No problem. Here's how it works.

What's Eligible

  • Items returned within 14 days of delivery.
  • Items that are unworn, unwashed, and unaltered with all original tags attached.
  • Items purchased at full price or during standard promotions.

What's Not Eligible

  • Clearance or flash-sale items (marked at checkout).
  • Accessories (earrings, bags) for hygiene reasons.
  • Items showing signs of wear, washing, or damage caused by the customer.

How to Initiate

Email returns@hawt.in with your order number, item name, and reason. We'll reply within 24 hours with a prepaid return label (Kolkata pickup) or instructions for courier return (other cities). Do not return items without prior authorisation — unauthorised returns will not be processed.

Exchange Process

We can exchange for a different size subject to availability. If the size you want is not available, we'll issue a full refund or store credit instead. Exchange shipping is free on the first request per order.

Refund Timeline

Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days of us receiving and inspecting the returned item. Card/UPI refunds go back to the original payment method. COD refunds are processed via NEFT.

How to Measure

  • Chest: Measure around the fullest part of your chest, keeping the tape horizontal.
  • Waist: Measure around your natural waist, the narrowest part of your torso.
  • Hips: Measure around the fullest part of your hips, approximately 20cm below your waist.
  • Shoulder: Measure from shoulder seam to shoulder seam across the back.

Our garments are designed for a contemporary, slightly fitted silhouette. If you prefer a more relaxed fit, size up. Product pages include specific fit notes where relevant.

— 06 / bulk orders

Bulk Orders

Corporate gifting, employee uniforms, event merchandise, college fests — we handle bulk orders for organisations and events.

Minimum Order Quantities

  • Standard catalogue items: Minimum 10 units per style/size.
  • Custom branding (embroidery/print): Minimum 25 units per design.
  • Fully custom product development: Minimum 100 units per style.

Pricing

Bulk orders receive tiered discounts: 10% off on 10–24 units, 15% on 25–49 units, 20% on 50+ units. Discounts apply to base product prices and cannot be combined with active site promotions.

Lead Time

Standard catalogue orders: 7–10 business days. Custom branding orders: 14–21 business days. Fully custom development: 45–60 business days from approved sample.
