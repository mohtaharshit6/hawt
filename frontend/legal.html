— 02 / privacy policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

We collect only what we need. We use it only to fulfil your order and improve your experience. We don't sell your data to anyone. Here's the full picture.

What We Collect

  • Account data: Name, email, password (hashed — we cannot read your password).
  • Order data: Delivery address, phone number, items ordered, payment status.
  • Usage data: Pages visited, products viewed, device and browser type (via server logs). No third-party analytics installed.

How We Use It

  • To process and fulfil your orders.
  • To send order confirmation and dispatch updates.
  • To notify you of restocks and new drops (only if you've opted in).
  • To improve the product catalogue and website based on aggregate browsing behaviour.

What We Don't Do

  • We do not sell or share your data with marketing agencies or advertisers.
  • We do not store payment card details — all card processing is handled by Razorpay on their PCI-DSS-compliant infrastructure.
  • We do not use cookies for tracking or retargeting ads.

Data Retention

Order data is retained for 7 years for accounting and tax purposes (as required under Indian GST law). Account data is retained until you request deletion. You can request deletion by emailing privacy@hawt.in.

Third-Party Services

We use Razorpay for payment processing. Their privacy policy is available at razorpay.com/privacy. We use standard courier partners for delivery; your address is shared with them only to the extent necessary for delivery.

Your Rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (India), you have the right to access, correct, and request deletion of your personal data. Contact us at privacy@hawt.in.

— 03 / shipping policy

Shipping Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Processing Time

Orders are processed within 1–2 business days of payment confirmation. Orders placed on Saturday, Sunday, or public holidays are processed the following business day.

Delivery Timelines

  • Kolkata: 1–2 business days after dispatch.
  • Metro cities (Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Pune): 3–5 business days after dispatch.
  • Rest of India: 5–8 business days after dispatch.
  • Remote / non-serviceable pincodes: Up to 10–12 business days. We'll notify you if your pincode falls into this category.

Shipping Charges

  • Orders above ₹2,499: Free shipping, no exceptions.
  • Orders below ₹2,499: Flat ₹99 shipping fee, charged at checkout.
  • Cash on Delivery: Additional ₹50 COD handling fee.

Tracking

Once your order is dispatched, you'll receive a tracking link via email. You can also track your order directly at orders page after logging in.

Failed Deliveries

If a delivery attempt fails due to an incorrect address or recipient unavailability, the courier will make two more attempts before returning the package to us. Re-dispatch will incur a fresh shipping charge. Please double-check your address before placing an order.

Damaged in Transit

If your order arrives visibly damaged, photograph the package before opening and contact us within 24 hours of delivery at support@hawt.in with photos. We'll arrange a replacement or refund at no cost to you.

— 04 / refund policy

Refund Policy

Last updated: June 2026

We stand behind everything we make. If something's wrong with your order, we'll make it right. Here's how.

Returns Window

You have 14 days from the date of delivery to initiate a return. Items must be unworn, unwashed, and in original condition with all tags attached.

Non-Returnable Items

  • Items purchased during Clearance or Flash Sale events (marked non-returnable at checkout).
  • Customised or personalised items.
  • Accessories (hygiene reasons).

Initiating a Return

Email returns@hawt.in with your order number and reason for return. We'll respond within 24 hours with a prepaid return label or pickup scheduling, depending on your location.

Refund Processing

  • Card/UPI payments: Refund to original payment method within 5–7 business days of us receiving the returned item.
  • Cash on Delivery orders: Refund via bank transfer (NEFT). We'll collect bank details over email.
  • Store credit: Available immediately upon return approval, if preferred over a monetary refund.

Exchanges

We offer free size exchanges subject to availability. Initiate through the same returns email. If your required size is out of stock, we'll issue a full refund or store credit.

Defective Items

If you receive a defective item (manufacturing defect, wrong item, damaged in transit), we'll arrange a free replacement or full refund with no return required. Simply email us a photo within 48 hours of delivery.
